- chosen: vṛtti-sārūpya = “conformity with the vṛtti-s,” a tatpuruṣa relation: vṛttīnām sārūpyam. (depends on commentary: **yes**)

**J4. The translation includes “[for the puruṣa]” because Vyāsa makes puruṣaḥ the bearer of the non-distinguished vṛtti and describes citta as the object/possession serving puruṣa, its master. This avoids making the sūtra say merely that the mind resembles its own modifications.**
