## Justifications

**J1. svapna and nidrā are not taken as one undifferentiated ‘dream-sleep’ object; the bhāṣya explicitly distributes them with vā ... vā.**
- chosen: The compound is to be unpacked as two alternatives: svapna-jñāna-ālambanam or nidrā-jñāna-ālambanam. (depends on commentary: **yes**)
- ys [1]: `svapnajñānālambanaṃ vā nidrājñānālambanaṃ vā tadākāraṃ yoginaś cittaṃ sthitipadaṃ labhata iti.` — ‘[It is] having dream-cognition as support, or having sleep-cognition as support; taking that form, the yogin’s mind attains the state of steadiness.’ The printed labhata is evidently to be understood in the sense of labhate.

**J2. ālambana is not rendered as an independent noun ‘a support’ here; it functions possessively with the yogin’s mind as the implicit qualified term.**
- chosen: svapnanidrājñānālambanam is an adjectival bahuvrīhi: ‘whose support is dream-cognition or sleep-cognition,’ qualifying understood cittaṃ. (depends on commentary: **yes**)
- ys [1]: `tadākāraṃ yoginaś cittaṃ sthitipadaṃ labhata iti.` — ‘Taking that form, the yogin’s mind attains the state of steadiness’—with labhata apparently standing for labhate.

**J3. The sūtra is elliptical; Vyāsa supplies the subject citta and the result sthitipadaṃ labhate, so the translation may include ‘the mind becomes steady’ in brackets.**
- chosen: The supplied sense is that the yogin’s mind becomes steady. (depends on commentary: **yes**)
- ys [1]: `yoginaś cittaṃ sthitipadaṃ labhata iti.` — ‘The yogin’s mind attains the state of steadiness’—again, labhata is read for sense as labhate.

**J4. Although svapna can denote sleep in general, the bhāṣya separates svapna-jñāna from nidrā-jñāna, so translating both as ‘sleep’ would erase the intended contrast.**
- chosen: svapna is rendered as ‘dream’ or ‘dream-state,’ contrasted with nidrā ‘sleep’. (depends on commentary: **yes**)
- ys [1]: `svapnajñānālambanaṃ vā nidrājñānālambanaṃ vā` — ‘Having dream-cognition as support, or having sleep-cognition as support.’

**J5. The sūtra’s final vā marks this as another optional means for steadiness, and Vyāsa’s internal expansion also uses vā ... vā between the two alternatives.**
- chosen: vā is the indeclinable ‘or’. (depends on commentary: **yes**)
- ys [1]: `svapnajñānālambanaṃ vā nidrājñānālambanaṃ vā` — ‘... as support, or ... as support.’

## Translation

> Or, [the yogin’s mind becomes steady when it is] one whose support (ālambana) is cognition (jñāna) connected with dream (svapna) or with sleep (nidrā).
