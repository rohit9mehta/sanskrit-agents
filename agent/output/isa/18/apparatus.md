## Justifications

**J1. The opening is a direct prayer to Agni, not a descriptive sentence about fire.**
- chosen: agne is a vocative address and naya is the imperative “lead/conduct.” (depends on commentary: **yes**)
- isup [1]: `he agne! naya gamaya supathā śobhanena mārgeṇa /` — “O Agni! naya means gamaya, ‘lead/conduct’; supathā means ‘by the fair path.’”

**J2. The compound is not merely “by a path well” or an unspecified route; Śaṅkara makes it a qualified path opposed to the dakṣiṇa-mārga characterized by repeated going and coming.**
- chosen: supathā is the instrumental “by the fair/good path,” specifically the path excluding the southern path of return; formally it is analyzed from the compound stem supath. (depends on commentary: **yes**)
- isup [1]: `he agne! naya gamaya supathā śobhanena mārgeṇa /` — “Lead/conduct by the fair path, by the beautiful/good road.”
- isup [2]: `supratheti viśeṣaṇaṃ dakṣiṇamārganivṛttyartham /` — “The adjective—printed here as supratheti, evidently unproofread for supatheti—is for excluding the southern path.”
- isup [3]: `nirviṇṇo 'haṃ dakṣiṇena mārgeṇa gatāgatalakṣaṇenāto yāce tvāṃ punaḥ punargamanāgamanavarjitena śobhanena pathā naya /` — “I am weary of the southern path, characterized by going and returning; therefore I ask you: lead by the good path free from repeated coming and going.”

**J3. The dative rāye is not taken in an unrelated sense such as “sound/noise”; in this bhāṣya it is the desired karmic fruit.**
- chosen: rāye means “for wealth,” explained as enjoyment of the fruit of action; asmān is “us” as qualified by the fruit of the dharma already taught. (depends on commentary: **yes**)
- isup [4]: `rāye dhanāya karmaphalabhogāyetyarthaḥ, asmānyathoktadharmaphalaviśiṣṭān viśvāni sarvāṇi he deva! vayunāni karmāṇi prajñānāni vā vidvāñjānan /` — “rāye means ‘for wealth,’ that is, for enjoyment of the fruit of action; asmān means ‘us, qualified by the fruit of the dharma stated above.’”

**J4. The object of Agni’s knowing is not another “path” but the totality of vayunāni as glossed by the commentator.**
- chosen: viśvāni qualifies vayunāni, and vayunāni are “actions, or cognitions/knowledges”; vidvān means “knowing.” (depends on commentary: **yes**)
- isup [4]: `rāye dhanāya karmaphalabhogāyetyarthaḥ, asmānyathoktadharmaphalaviśiṣṭān viśvāni sarvāṇi he deva! vayunāni karmāṇi prajñānāni vā vidvāñjānan /` — “viśvāni means ‘all’; O god, vayunāni are actions, or cognitions; vidvān means knowing.”

**J5. juhurāṇam is construed adjectivally with enas, and asmat is separative: the sin is to be removed from us.**
- chosen: yuyodhi asks Agni to separate/remove or destroy from us the crooked, deceitful sin (enas). (depends on commentary: **yes**)
- isup [5]: `kiñca yuyodhi viyojaya vināśaya asmadasmatto juhurāṇaṃ kuṭilaṃ vañcanātmakamenaḥ pāpam /` — “Further, yuyodhi means separate, destroy; asmat means from us; juhurāṇam means crooked, deceitful; enas means sin.”
- isup [6]: `tato vayaṃ viśuddhāḥ santa iṣṭaṃ prāpsyāma ityabhiprāyaḥ /` — “Then, being purified, we shall obtain what is desired—this is the intention.”

**J6. The final pāda is not “your salutation and speech we make” but a promise/prayer to perform verbal homage to Agni, since fuller service is not now possible.**
- chosen: te is dative “to you”; nama uktiṃ is a single “utterance of obeisance”; vidhema is optative, “may we render/serve with salutation.” (depends on commentary: **yes**)
- isup [7]: `kintu vayamidānīṃ te na śaknumaḥ paricaryāṃ kartum /` — “But now we are not able to perform service to you.”
- isup [8]: `bhūyiṣṭhāṃ bahutarāṃ te tubhyaṃ nama uktiṃ namaskāravacanaṃ vidhema namaskāreṇa paricarema ityarthaḥ /` — “bhūyiṣṭhām means more/very abundant; te means to you; nama uktiṃ means an utterance of obeisance; vidhema means we would serve with salutation.”

## Translation

> O Agni, lead us—qualified by the fruit of the dharma already taught—by the fair path, the path free from repeated going and coming, to wealth, that is, enjoyment of action’s fruit;
O god, knowing all the vayunāni, all actions or forms of knowledge.
Remove from us the crooked, deceitful sin (enas);
to you may we render a most abundant utterance of obeisance (nama-ukti).

## Analyzer disagreements

- ByT5 split su and pathā; I analyze supathā as one compound stem supath in the instrumental singular, since Śaṅkara glosses it as śobhanena mārgeṇa and treats it as a viśeṣaṇa. The formal compound type is recorded as broad tatpuruṣa/prādi-tatpuruṣa rather than the earlier karmadhāraya label.
- ByT5 used mad as the lemma for asmān and asmat and did not supply gender; I use the grammatical pronominal stem asmad and supply contextual/default masculine linga while noting that first-person pronouns are not gender-contrastive.
- ByT5 treated namaḥ and uktim as two separate inflected words; I analyze the printed nama uktiṃ as the compound nama-uktiṃ because Śaṅkara glosses it as a single namaskāravacanaṃ.
- For participial vidvān, I give the inflectional nominal stem vidvat rather than the tagger’s verbal lemma vid; the semantic derivation from √vid is retained.
- For participial juhurāṇam, I give the nominal stem juhurāṇa rather than only the verbal root hvṛ/hvar; the tagger’s root identification is retained in the note.
- For te, ByT5 did not supply gender; I supply masculine by reference to the masculine addressee Agni, while noting that the second-person pronoun is not gender-contrastive.

## One-shot delta

- A commentary-blind translation would likely render rāye simply as “wealth”; Śaṅkara narrows it to karmaphalabhoga, enjoyment of the fruit of action.
- It might miss that supathā is polemically contrasted with the dakṣiṇa-mārga, the southern path of repeated return, and not just any pleasant road.
- It might translate vayunāni vaguely as “ways” or “paths”; Śaṅkara glosses them as karmāṇi or prajñānāni, actions or cognitions.
- It might split nama uktiṃ into two objects or take te as possessive; Śaṅkara takes te as tubhyam and nama uktiṃ as one namaskāravacana, an utterance of obeisance.
- It might miss the force of yuyodhi and juhurāṇam: the prayer is to remove/destroy from us a crooked, deceitful sin, yielding purification.

## Open questions

- Commentary line 2 reads supratheti; given the source caveat and context, this has been treated as an unproofread form for supatheti, but the quoted line is left verbatim.
- The mūla prints nama uktiṃ as two graphic words; the apparatus analyzes it as the compound sense nama-uktiṃ because Śaṅkara glosses it with the single expression namaskāravacanaṃ.
- Vidyut-prakriya did not derive yuyodhi under the ordinary √yu paradigms it tried; this apparatus retains yuyodhi as a Vedic 2nd singular imperative because both the lexical evidence and Śaṅkara’s gloss require it.
