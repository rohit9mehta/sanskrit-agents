- isup [1]: `hiraṇmayamiva hiraṇmayaṃ jyotirmayamityetat /` — “Hiraṇmaya means as though made of gold; that is, made of light.”

## Translation

> By a golden-bright, light-filled vessel
is covered the face—the doorway—of Truth (satya).
That, O Pūṣan, uncover; remove it
for me, whose dharma (dharma) is truth,
for the seeing, the realization, of your true Self.

## One-shot delta

- A commentary-blind translation might render hiraṇmayena pātreṇa as simply “with a golden vessel,” missing Śaṅkara’s “light-filled” and “covering” interpretation.
- It might translate satyasya mukham literally as “the mouth of truth,” missing the gloss mukhaṃ dvāram and the identification with Brahman in the solar disk.
- It might take satyadharmāya as “for true dharma,” but Śaṅkara construes it personally: “for me whose dharma is truth,” with an alternative “for the performer of dharma as it really is.”
