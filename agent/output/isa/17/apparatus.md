- isup [1]: `athedānīṃ mama mariṣyato vāyuḥ prāṇo 'dhyātmaparicchedaṃ hitvādhidaivatātmānaṃ sarvātmakamanilamamṛtaṃ sūtrātmānaṃ pratipadyatāmiti vākyaśeṣaḥ /` — ‘Now, of me as I am dying, vāyu—the breath—having abandoned its individual limitation, may attain the immortal Anila, the adhidaivata self, the all-self, the sūtrātman’: this is the completion of the sentence.

## Translation

> May my breath (vāyu), as I am dying, leaving its individual limitation, attain the immortal cosmic Air (anila); [may this subtle body (liṅga), shaped by knowledge and action, depart.] Then may this body, consigned to fire, end in ashes. Om—the symbol of Brahman called Agni. O Agni-as-Resolve (kratu), remember; remember what has been done. O Agni-as-Resolve, remember; remember what has been done.

## Analyzer disagreements

- amṛtam: ByT5 tagged it as neuter accusative; I analyze it as masculine accusative singular, agreeing with masculine anilam, because Śaṅkara construes anilam amṛtam as a single accusative object (line 1).
- idam, bhasmāntam, and śarīram: ByT5 tagged the forms as neuter accusative; I analyze them as neuter nominative singular, since Śaṅkara supplies bhūyāt and makes idaṃ śarīram the subject and bhasmāntam the predicate (line 3).
- bhasmāntam: ByT5 split bhasma + antam; I analyze the whole inflected form as the bahuvrīhi compound stem bhasmānta, ‘having ashes as its end’, predicated of śarīram (line 3).
- kṛtam, both occurrences: ByT5 lemmatizes to the verbal root kṛ/participle; I record the nominal participial stem kṛta as a neuter accusative singular substantive, because Śaṅkara glosses it with anuṣṭhitaṃ karma, the action performed (line 6).

## One-shot delta
