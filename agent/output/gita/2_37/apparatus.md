## Translation

> If slain, you will attain heaven (svarga);
or, having conquered, you will enjoy the earth.
Therefore arise, O son of Kuntī (Kaunteya),
for battle, with your resolve (niścaya) made.

## One-shot delta

- Without Śaṅkara, one might take hataḥ weakly as ‘struck’ or even construe it actively; the bhāṣya’s hataḥ san and mariṣyāmi vā make it the ‘slain/die’ alternative.
- A commentary-blind rendering might say simply ‘having conquered, enjoy the earth’; Śaṅkara specifies that what is conquered is Karṇa and other warriors, while the earth is enjoyed afterward.
- The verse’s logic could be missed as mere either/or prediction; Śaṅkara states that both alternatives are gain for Arjuna.
- kṛta-niścayaḥ might be flattened to ‘determined’; Śaṅkara gives the concrete resolve: ‘I shall conquer the enemies, or I shall die.’
