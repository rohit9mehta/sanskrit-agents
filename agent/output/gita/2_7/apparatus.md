## Translation

> With my own disposition stricken by the fault of weakness (kārpaṇya),
with my mind bewildered about duty (dharma), I ask you:
what would be the higher good (śreyas)—tell that to me decisively.
I am your disciple; instruct me, who have taken refuge in you.
