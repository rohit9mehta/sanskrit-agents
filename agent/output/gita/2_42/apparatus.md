**J3. The word is a privative adjective: ‘not vipaścit,’ glossed by Śaṅkara as lacking intelligence and discrimination.**

## Translation

> That flowery speech—pleasing to hear—which the undiscerning proclaim,
O Pārtha:
delighting in Veda-speech (veda-vāda),
saying, “There is nothing else [than works that bring fruits such as heaven].”
