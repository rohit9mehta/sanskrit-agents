## Translation

> For the wise, endowed with the buddhi of equanimity, having abandoned the action-born fruit—the obtaining of desirable and undesirable bodies—are freed, even while living, from the bondage that is birth, and go to the affliction-free station (pada), the supreme station of Viṣṇu called liberation (mokṣa).

## One-shot delta

- A commentary-blind translation could attach karmajam to the nearer buddhi-yuktāḥ and produce “action-born intelligence”; Śaṅkara explicitly connects karmajaṃ with phalaṃ despite the separation.
- It could render buddhi-yuktāḥ as merely “intelligent” or “rational”; the bhāṣya makes it samatva-buddhi, the buddhi of equanimity.
- It could take janma-bandha as a loose “bond of birth”; Śaṅkara specifies that birth itself is bondage and that the release is while living.
- It could translate padaṃ anāmayam as a generic “disease-free place/step”; the bhāṣya identifies it as Viṣṇu’s supreme pada, called mokṣa, free from all afflictions.
