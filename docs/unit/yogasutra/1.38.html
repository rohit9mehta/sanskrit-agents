Yoga-sūtra with Vyāsa's bhāṣya · 1.38

37 svapnanidrājñānālambanaṃ vā

Translation. Or, [the yogin’s mind becomes steady when it is] one whose support (ālambana) is cognition (jñāna) connected with dream (svapna) or with sleep (nidrā).

How the commentary settled it

Where the verse alone is ambiguous, the reading follows Vyāsa's commentary; the deciding lines are quoted below each decision.
svapna and nidrā are not taken as one undifferentiated ‘dream-sleep’ object; the bhāṣya explicitly distributes them with vā ... vā.
→ The compound is to be unpacked as two alternatives: svapna-jñāna-ālambanam or nidrā-jñāna-ālambanam.
ys [1]svapnajñānālambanaṃ vā nidrājñānālambanaṃ vā tadākāraṃ yoginaś cittaṃ sthitipadaṃ labhata iti.
‘[It is] having dream-cognition as support, or having sleep-cognition as support; taking that form, the yogin’s mind attains the state of steadiness.’ The printed labhata is evidently to be understood in the sense of labhate.
ālambana is not rendered as an independent noun ‘a support’ here; it functions possessively with the yogin’s mind as the implicit qualified term.
→ svapnanidrājñānālambanam is an adjectival bahuvrīhi: ‘whose support is dream-cognition or sleep-cognition,’ qualifying understood cittaṃ.
ys [1]tadākāraṃ yoginaś cittaṃ sthitipadaṃ labhata iti.
‘Taking that form, the yogin’s mind attains the state of steadiness’—with labhata apparently standing for labhate.
The sūtra is elliptical; Vyāsa supplies the subject citta and the result sthitipadaṃ labhate, so the translation may include ‘the mind becomes steady’ in brackets.
→ The supplied sense is that the yogin’s mind becomes steady.
ys [1]yoginaś cittaṃ sthitipadaṃ labhata iti.
‘The yogin’s mind attains the state of steadiness’—again, labhata is read for sense as labhate.
Although svapna can denote sleep in general, the bhāṣya separates svapna-jñāna from nidrā-jñāna, so translating both as ‘sleep’ would erase the intended contrast.
→ svapna is rendered as ‘dream’ or ‘dream-state,’ contrasted with nidrā ‘sleep’.
ys [1]svapnajñānālambanaṃ vā nidrājñānālambanaṃ vā
‘Having dream-cognition as support, or having sleep-cognition as support.’
The sūtra’s final vā marks this as another optional means for steadiness, and Vyāsa’s internal expansion also uses vā ... vā between the two alternatives.
→ vā is the indeclinable ‘or’.
ys [1]svapnajñānālambanaṃ vā nidrājñānālambanaṃ vā
‘... as support, or ... as support.’
