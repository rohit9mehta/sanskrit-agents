Yoga-sūtra with Vyāsa's bhāṣya · 1.41

kṣīṇavṛtter abhijātasyeva maṇer grahītṛgrahaṇagrāhyeṣu tatsthatadañjanatā samāpattiḥ

Translation. For the mind whose fluctuation (vṛtti) has subsided, as with a clear crystal-jewel, coalescence (samāpatti) is its taking on their form while it is situated among the grasper (grahītṛ), the grasping-instruments (grahaṇa), and the graspables (grāhya).

How the commentary settled it

Where the verse alone is ambiguous, the reading follows Vyāsa's commentary; the deciding lines are quoted below each decision.
The form is taken morphologically as a feminine genitive singular karmadhāraya, while Vyāsa’s artha-gloss and later mention of cetas determine that the relevant bearer is the mind.
→ Grammatically, kṣīṇavṛtteḥ is ‘of the subsided vṛtti’; in the sūtra’s commentarial sense, it describes the mind whose vṛtti/pratyaya has subsided.
ys [1]kṣīṇavṛtter iti pratyastamitapratyayasyety arthaḥ.
‘kṣīṇavṛtti’ means ‘whose pratyaya has set/subsided’ or ‘of the subsided pratyaya.’
ys [11]abhijātamaṇikalpasya cetaso
Vyāsa explicitly supplies the referent as ‘of the mind comparable to a fine jewel.’
The phrase is not an additional doctrinal subject; Vyāsa marks it as a dṛṣṭānta and explains its point through the crystal’s taking on the color/form of its support.
→ abhijātasya maṇer iva is an illustrative simile of a clear crystal-jewel.
ys [2]abhijātasyeva maṇer iti dṛṣṭāntopādānam.
‘Like a fine jewel’ is the introduction of an example.
ys [3]yathā sphaṭika upāśrayabhedāt tattadrūpoparakta upāśrayarūpākāreṇa nirbhāsate tathā grāhyālambanoparaktaṃ cittaṃ grāhyasamāpannaṃ grāhyasvarūpākāreṇa nirbhāsate.
As a crystal, depending on different supports, is colored by this or that form and appears in the form of the support, so the mind colored by a graspable support appears in the graspable’s own form.
The compound is taken as a locative plural dvandva naming the possible supports in which the mind’s samāpatti occurs.
→ grahītṛ-grahaṇa-grāhya denotes the triad: grasper/puruṣa, grasping-instruments/indriyas, and graspable objects/bhūtas.
ys [11]grahītṛgrahaṇagrāhyeṣu puruṣendriyabhūteṣu
Vyāsa glosses ‘in the grasper, grasping, and graspable’ as ‘in puruṣa, senses, and elements.’
ys [7]tathā grahaṇeṣv apīndriyeṣv api draṣṭavyam.
In the case of grahaṇas, it is to be understood with respect to the senses too.
ys [9]tathā grahītṛpuruṣālambanoparaktaṃ grahītṛpuruṣasamāpannaṃ grahītṛpuruṣasvarūpākāreṇa nirbhāsate.
When colored by the support that is the grahītṛ-puruṣa, it has entered into the grahītṛ-puruṣa and appears in that form.
tat-stha is read as ‘situated in them’ and tad-añjanatā as taking on their form/color, not literal smearing or collyrium. The compound relation is represented as tatsthasya tadañjanatā, following Vyāsa’s teṣu sthitasya tadākārāpattiḥ.
→ tatsthatadañjanatā is ‘the assumption of their form by what is situated in them.’
ys [11]yā tatsthatadañjanatā teṣu sthitasya tadākārāpattiḥ sā samāpattir ity ucyate.
That tat-stha-tad-añjanatā is the assumption of that form by what is situated in them; that is called samāpatti.
ys [8]grahaṇālambanoparaktaṃ grahaṇasamāpannaṃ grahaṇasvarūpākāreṇa nirbhāsate.
Colored by a grahaṇa-support and coalesced with grahaṇa, it appears in the form of grahaṇa.
Vyāsa repeatedly explains samāpatti with samāpanna and svarūpākāreṇa nirbhāsate: the mind, colored by a support, appears in that support’s own form. The sūtra’s predicate defines this as samāpatti.
→ samāpatti is rendered as cognitive ‘coalescence’ or engrossed conformity, not as accident or mere meeting.
ys [3]grāhyālambanoparaktaṃ cittaṃ grāhyasamāpannaṃ grāhyasvarūpākāreṇa nirbhāsate.
The mind, colored by a graspable support and coalesced with the graspable, appears in the graspable’s own form.
ys [11]sā samāpattir ity ucyate.
That is called samāpatti.

Vyāsa's commentary on this unit

1kṣīṇavṛtter iti pratyastamitapratyayasyety arthaḥ.
2abhijātasyeva maṇer iti dṛṣṭāntopādānam.
3yathā sphaṭika upāśrayabhedāt tattadrūpoparakta upāśrayarūpākāreṇa nirbhāsate tathā grāhyālambanoparaktaṃ cittaṃ grāhyasamāpannaṃ grāhyasvarūpākāreṇa nirbhāsate.
4bhūtasūkṣmoparaktaṃ bhūtasūkṣmasamāpannaṃ bhūtasūkṣmasvarūpābhāsaṃ bhavati.
5tathā sthūlālambanoparaktaṃ sthūlarūpasamāpannaṃ sthūlarūpābhāsaṃ bhavati.
6tathā viśvabhedoparaktaṃ viśvabhedasamāpannaṃ viśvarūpābhāsaṃ bhavati.
7tathā grahaṇeṣv apīndriyeṣv api draṣṭavyam.
8grahaṇālambanoparaktaṃ grahaṇasamāpannaṃ grahaṇasvarūpākāreṇa nirbhāsate.
9tathā grahītṛpuruṣālambanoparaktaṃ grahītṛpuruṣasamāpannaṃ grahītṛpuruṣasvarūpākāreṇa nirbhāsate.
10tathā muktapuruṣālambanoparaktaṃ muktapuruṣasamāpannaṃ muktapuruṣasvarūpākāreṇa nirbhāsata iti.
11tad evam abhijātamaṇikalpasya cetaso grahītṛgrahaṇagrāhyeṣu puruṣendriyabhūteṣu yā tatsthatadañjanatā teṣu sthitasya tadākārāpattiḥ sā samāpattir ity ucyate.
