Yoga-sūtra with Vyāsa's bhāṣya · 1.43

smṛtipariśuddhau svarūpaśūnyevārthamātranirbhāsā nirvitarkā

Translation. Upon the purification of memory, [the samāpatti], as if devoid of its own form, shining forth as the object alone, is nirvitarkā (without vitarka).

How the commentary settled it

Where the verse alone is ambiguous, the reading follows Vyāsa's commentary; the deciding lines are quoted below each decision.
smṛtipariśuddhau is not an attributive ‘purified memory’ but a locative setting for the arising of this samāpatti: when that purification is present, the prajñā is called nirvitarkā.
→ ‘Upon/when memory is purified’ as a locative condition.
ys [1]yā śabdasaṃketaśrutānumānajñānavikalpasmṛtipariśuddhau grāhyasvarūpa.uparaktā prajñā svam iva prajñāsvarūpaṃ grahaṇātmakaṃ tyaktvā padārthamātrasvarūpā grāhyasvarūpāpanneva bhavati sā tadā nirvitarkā samāpattiḥ.
That cognition which, when memory involving verbal convention, testimony, inference, and conceptualized knowledge has been purified, is colored by the form of the graspable object, as it were abandoning its own cognitive form whose nature is grasping, and becomes of the mere object’s form, as though having taken on the graspable object’s form—that then is nirvitarkā samāpatti.
The sūtra’s subject is supplied from the prior sūtra and made explicit by Vyāsa: the prajñā in such concentration is ‘then nirvitarkā samāpatti’. Hence svarūpaśūnyā, arthamātranirbhāsā, and nirvitarkā are nominative feminine singular predicates of that understood samāpatti/prajñā.
→ The feminine nominatives describe an understood samāpatti, mediated by prajñā.
ys [1]yā śabdasaṃketaśrutānumānajñānavikalpasmṛtipariśuddhau grāhyasvarūpa.uparaktā prajñā svam iva prajñāsvarūpaṃ grahaṇātmakaṃ tyaktvā padārthamātrasvarūpā grāhyasvarūpāpanneva bhavati sā tadā nirvitarkā samāpattiḥ.
That cognition ... becomes of the mere object’s form ... that then is nirvitarkā samāpatti.
Vyāsa’s gloss makes sva-rūpa the prajñā-svarūpa, specifically grahaṇātmaka, ‘whose nature is apprehension/grasping’; and he preserves the force of iva by saying svam iva ... tyaktvā and grāhyasvarūpāpannā iva. The translation therefore says ‘as if devoid of its own form,’ not simply ‘empty of essence’.
→ ‘As if devoid of its own form’ means as if lacking the cognition’s own grasping-form, not ontological non-existence.
ys [1]svam iva prajñāsvarūpaṃ grahaṇātmakaṃ tyaktvā padārthamātrasvarūpā grāhyasvarūpāpanneva bhavati
as it were abandoning its own form, the form of cognition, whose nature is grasping, it becomes of the mere object’s form, as though having taken on the graspable object’s form
The bhāṣya glosses the state as padārthamātrasvarūpā and grāhyasvarūpāpannā, and later says the avayavī becomes the viṣayin of nirvitarkā samāpatti. Thus ‘object alone’ is the safest translation.
→ artha in arthamātranirbhāsā is the graspable object/padārtha, not ‘purpose’ or merely verbal ‘meaning’.
ys [1]padārthamātrasvarūpā grāhyasvarūpāpanneva bhavati
it becomes of the mere object’s form, as though having taken on the form of the graspable object
ys [11]tasmād asty avayavī yo mahattvādivyavahārāpannaḥ samāpatter nirvitarkāyā viṣayī bhavati.
Therefore there is a whole, an avayavī, which is the basis of practical dealings such as largeness and becomes the object of nirvitarkā samāpatti.
Rather than using MW’s general ‘unreflecting/inconsiderate’, the bhāṣya explicitly identifies the described state as nirvitarkā samāpatti. The translation therefore preserves the technical term and gives only the literal orientation ‘without vitarka’.
→ nirvitarkā is a technical name of the samāpatti, rendered ‘without vitarka’ and retained in Sanskrit.
ys [1]sā tadā nirvitarkā samāpattiḥ.
that then is nirvitarkā samāpatti.
The external sandhi is grammatical: final ā of śūnyā with initial i of iva gives e, and final a of iva with initial a of artha gives ā, producing śūnyevārtha-.
→ The printed sequence resolves as svarūpaśūnyā iva arthamātranirbhāsā.

Vyāsa's commentary on this unit

1yā śabdasaṃketaśrutānumānajñānavikalpasmṛtipariśuddhau grāhyasvarūpa.uparaktā prajñā svam iva prajñāsvarūpaṃ grahaṇātmakaṃ tyaktvā padārthamātrasvarūpā grāhyasvarūpāpanneva bhavati sā tadā nirvitarkā samāpattiḥ.
2tathā ca vyākyātaṃ tasyā ekabuddhyupakramo hy arthātmāṇupracayaviśeṣātmā gavādir ghaṭādir vā lokaḥ.
3sa ca saṃsthānaviśeṣo bhūtasūkṣmāṇāṃ sādhāraṇo dharma ātmabhūtaḥ phalena vyaktenānumitaḥ svavyañjakāñjanaḥ prādurbhavati.
4dharmāntarasya kapālāder udaye ca tirobhavati.
5sa eṣa dharmo 'vayavīty ucyate.
6yo 'sāv ekaś ca mahāṃś cāṇīṃyaś ca sparśavāṃś ca kriyādharmakaś cānityaś ca tenāvayavinā vyavahārāḥ kriyante.
7yasya punar avastukaḥ sa pracayaviśeṣaḥ.
8sūkṣmaṃ ca kāraṇam anupalabhyam avikalpasya tasyāvayavyabhāvād atadrūpapratiṣṭhaṃ mithyājñānam iti prāyeṇa sarvam eva prāptaṃ mithyājñānam iti.
9tadā ca samyagjñānam api kiṃ syād viṣayābhāvāt.
10yad yad upalabhyate tat tad avayavitvenāmnātam.
11tasmād asty avayavī yo mahattvādivyavahārāpannaḥ samāpatter nirvitarkāyā viṣayī bhavati.
