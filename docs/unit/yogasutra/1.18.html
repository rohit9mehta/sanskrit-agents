Yoga-sūtra with Vyāsa's bhāṣya · 1.18

virāmapratyayābhyāsapūrvaḥ saṃskāraśeṣo 'nyaḥ

Translation. The other [samādhi], preceded by the practice (abhyāsa) of the cessation-cognition (virāma-pratyaya), has only latent impressions (saṃskāra) remaining.

How the commentary settled it

Where the verse alone is ambiguous, the reading follows Vyāsa's commentary; the deciding lines are quoted below each decision.
Resolve the implied subject and referent of anyaḥ.
→ anyaḥ means the other samādhi, namely asaṃprajñāta/nirbīja samādhi, described as nirodha of the mind.
ys [1]sarvavṛttipratyastamaye saṃskāraśeṣo nirodhaś cittasya samādhir asaṃprajñātaḥ.
When all mental activities have set, the mind’s nirodha, with saṃskāra as remainder, is asaṃprajñāta samādhi.
ys [5]tadabhyāsapūrvakaṃ hi cittaṃ nirālambanam abhāvaprāptam iva bhavatīty eṣa nirbījaḥ samādhir asaṃprajñātaḥ.
For the mind, preceded by practice of that, becomes supportless, as though it had reached absence; this is seedless samādhi, asaṃprajñāta.
Determine the sense of virāmapratyaya.
→ virāma-pratyaya is the objectless/contentless cessation-cognition taken as an ālambana, not an ordinary object-supported idea or merely a ‘belief’.
ys [3]sālambano hy abhyāsas tatsādhanāya na kalpata iti virāmapratyayo nirvastuka ālambanīkriyate.
For object-supported practice is not fit as a means to it; therefore the virāma-pratyaya, objectless, is made the support.
ys [4]sa cārthaśūnyaḥ.
And it is empty of an object/content.
ys [1]sarvavṛttipratyastamaye saṃskāraśeṣo nirodhaś cittasya samādhir asaṃprajñātaḥ.

Construe the compound relation of abhyāsa-pūrva in virāmapratyayābhyāsapūrvaḥ.
→ virāmapratyayābhyāsapūrvaḥ means ‘preceded by, or having as its prior condition, the practice of the virāma-pratyaya’.
ys [5]tadabhyāsapūrvakaṃ hi cittaṃ nirālambanam abhāvaprāptam iva bhavatīty eṣa nirbījaḥ samādhir asaṃprajñātaḥ.

Interpret saṃskāraśeṣaḥ and its scope.
→ saṃskāraśeṣaḥ is bahuvrīhi in force: the samādhi/nirodha has saṃskāra as the remaining residue after all vṛttis cease; it is not total annihilation.
