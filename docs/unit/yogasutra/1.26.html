How the commentary settled it

Where the verse alone is ambiguous, the reading follows Vyāsa's commentary; the deciding lines are quoted below each decision.
Although guru can mean “heavy” in ordinary lexicon, the bhāṣya explicitly discusses pūrve guravaḥ, “former teachers,” and restates the sūtra as sa eṣa pūrveṣām api guruḥ. Thus the nominative guruḥ is “teacher,” not an adjective “heavy,” and pūrveṣām is read substantively with “teachers” understood.
→ guruḥ = “teacher,” and pūrveṣām = “of the former teachers”
ys [1]pūrve hi guravaḥ kālenāvacchidyante.
For former teachers are delimited by time.
ys [2]yatrāvacchedārthena kālo nopāvartate sa eṣa pūrveṣām api guruḥ.
Where time does not come into operation for the purpose of delimitation, that very one is the teacher even of the former ones.
The commentary repeats the phrase as pūrveṣām api guruḥ, making api inclusive/emphatic with the genitive group: he is teacher not only of later beings but even of prior teachers.
→ api scopes with pūrveṣām: “even of the former [teachers]”
ys [2]sa eṣa pūrveṣām api guruḥ
that very one is the teacher even of the former ones
The instrumental kālena is tied to avaccheda: ordinary former teachers are kālena avacchidyante, “delimited by time,” whereas for this one time does not operate avacchedārthena, “for delimitation.” The ablative anavacchedāt therefore gives the reason for his being guru even of the former teachers.
→ kālena anavacchedāt = “because he is not delimited by time”
ys [1]pūrve hi guravaḥ kālenāvacchidyante.
For former teachers are delimited by time.
ys [2]yatrāvacchedārthena kālo nopāvartate
where time does not come into operation for the purpose of delimitation
Vyāsa extends the point from the beginning of the present creation to the beginnings of past creations; therefore the “former” teachers are not only immediate predecessors within one historical sequence.
→ pūrve includes earlier cosmic cycles, not merely historically earlier persons in this creation
ys [3]yathāsya sargasyādau prakarṣagatyā siddhas tathātikrāntasargādiṣv api pratyetavyaḥ.
Just as he is established at the beginning of this creation by the course of pre-eminence, so too he is to be recognized at the beginnings of past creations.
