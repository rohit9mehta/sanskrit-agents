How the commentary settled it

Where the verse alone is ambiguous, the reading follows Vyāsa's commentary; the deciding lines are quoted below each decision.
Translate īśvarapraṇidhānāt as ‘through devotion to Īśvara’.
→ praṇidhāna is ‘devotion’, specifically bhaktiviśeṣa, not merely ‘fixing’, ‘application’, or ‘exertion’.
ys [1]praṇidhānād bhaktiviśeṣād āvarjita īśvaras tam a
‘By praṇidhāna, by a special devotion, Īśvara, won over, [him …]’; the supplied bhāṣya line breaks off at tam a.
Analyze īśvarapraṇidhāna as a tatpuruṣa: īśvare praṇidhānam, ‘devotion directed to Īśvara’.
→ īśvara is the object/recipient of the devotion expressed by the compound.
ys [1]praṇidhānād bhaktiviśeṣād āvarjita īśvaras tam a
The bhāṣya glosses praṇidhāna as a special devotion and says Īśvara is ‘won over’, indicating that Īśvara is the one toward whom the devotion is directed.
Render the pañcamī īśvarapraṇidhānāt as ‘through devotion to Īśvara’.
→ The ablative singular expresses means/cause: ‘through’ or ‘by reason of’.
ys [1]praṇidhānād bhaktiviśeṣād
The bhāṣya repeats the ablative: ‘by/from praṇidhāna, by/from a special devotion’.
Place ‘or’ first in English to match the sūtra’s alternative construction: ‘Or, through …’.
→ vā is the indeclinable disjunctive ‘or’.
