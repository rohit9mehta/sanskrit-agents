Yoga-sūtra with Vyāsa's bhāṣya · 1.50

tajjaḥ saṃskāro 'nyasaṃskārapratibandhī

Translation. The latent impression (saṃskāra) born of that [samādhi-prajñā] obstructs the other latent impressions—the vyutthāna-saṃskāras.

How the commentary settled it

Where the verse alone is ambiguous, the reading follows Vyāsa's commentary; the deciding lines are quoted below each decision.
The pronoun tad in the compound is not left as a vague “that”; Vyāsa identifies the source as samādhi-prajñā, and then describes the succeeding saṃskāras as prajñā-made.
→ tajjaḥ = born from samādhi-prajñā / produced by that prajñā
ys [1]samādhiprajñāprabhavaḥ saṃskāro vyutthānasaṃskārāśayaṃ bādhate.
The saṃskāra whose origin is samādhi-prajñā blocks the store of vyutthāna-saṃskāras.
ys [4]tataḥ samādhijā prajñā, tataḥ prajñākṛtāḥ saṃskārā iti navo navaḥ saṃskārāśayo jāyate.
From that comes prajñā born of samādhi; from that, saṃskāras made by prajñā—thus a continually new store of saṃskāras arises.
The rendering “latent impression” is chosen over a generic “formation” because Vyāsa places these saṃskāras in an āśaya and says that pratyayas arise from them unless they are overpowered.
→ saṃskāra as a latent impression/disposition that can produce pratyayas
ys [1, 2]samādhiprajñāprabhavaḥ saṃskāro vyutthānasaṃskārāśayaṃ bādhate. vyutthānasaṃskārābhibhavāt tatprabhavāḥ pratyayā na bhavanti.
The saṃskāra born from samādhi-prajñā blocks the store of vyutthāna-saṃskāras. Because the vyutthāna-saṃskāras are overpowered, the pratyayas produced by them do not occur.
The “other” saṃskāras are specified by the bhāṣya as vyutthāna-saṃskāras, not the prajñā-made saṃskāras themselves nor every saṃskāra without distinction.
→ anya-saṃskāras = vyutthāna-saṃskāras, the other/ordinary emerging dispositions
ys [1, 2]samādhiprajñāprabhavaḥ saṃskāro vyutthānasaṃskārāśayaṃ bādhate. vyutthānasaṃskārābhibhavāt tatprabhavāḥ pratyayā na bhavanti.
The saṃskāra born from samādhi-prajñā blocks the store of vyutthāna-saṃskāras. Because the vyutthāna-saṃskāras are overpowered, the pratyayas produced by them do not occur.
Although pratibandhin can mean either impeded or impeding in different contexts, here Vyāsa recasts the sūtra with the active finite verb bādhate and explains the result as abhibhava, overpowering, of the vyutthāna-saṃskāras.
→ pratibandhī is active: “obstructing/blocking”
ys [1, 2]samādhiprajñāprabhavaḥ saṃskāro vyutthānasaṃskārāśayaṃ bādhate. vyutthānasaṃskārābhibhavāt tatprabhavāḥ pratyayā na bhavanti.
The saṃskāra born from samādhi-prajñā blocks the store of vyutthāna-saṃskāras. Because the vyutthāna-saṃskāras are overpowered, the pratyayas produced by them do not occur.
The bhāṣya explicitly rejects the worry that this superior saṃskāra would make the citta sādhikāra, fit for further purposive activity; rather, because it conduces to kleśa-destruction, it withdraws the citta from its own activity.
→ the prajñā-made saṃskāra does not bind the citta to further ordinary activity
ys [7, 8]na te prajñākṛtāḥ saṃskārāḥ kleśakṣayahetutvāc cittam adhikāraviśiṣṭaṃ kurvanti. cittaṃ hi te svakāryād avasādayanti.
No: those prajñā-made saṃskāras, because they are a cause of the destruction of the kleśas, do not make the citta qualified for further function. Rather, they cause the citta to subside from its own activity.

Vyāsa's commentary on this unit

1samādhiprajñāprabhavaḥ saṃskāro vyutthānasaṃskārāśayaṃ bādhate.
2vyutthānasaṃskārābhibhavāt tatprabhavāḥ pratyayā na bhavanti.
3pratyayanirodhe samādhir upatiṣṭhate.
4tataḥ samādhijā prajñā, tataḥ prajñākṛtāḥ saṃskārā iti navo navaḥ saṃskārāśayo jāyate.
5tataś ca prajñā, tataś ca saṃskārā iti.
6katham asau saṃskārātiśayaś cittaṃ sādhikāraṃ na kariṣyatīti.
7na te prajñākṛtāḥ saṃskārāḥ kleśakṣayahetutvāc cittam adhikāraviśiṣṭaṃ kurvanti.
8cittaṃ hi te svakāryād avasādayanti.
9khyātiparyavasānaṃ hi cittaceṣṭitam iti.
