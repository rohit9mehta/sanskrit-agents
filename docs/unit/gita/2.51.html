Bhagavad-Gītā with Śaṅkara's bhāṣya · 2.51

yasmāt - karmajaṃ buddhi-yuktā hi phalaṃ tyaktvā manīṣiṇaḥ | janma-bandha-vinirmuktāḥ padaṃ gacchanty anāmayam

Translation. For the wise, endowed with the buddhi of equanimity, having abandoned the action-born fruit—the obtaining of desirable and undesirable bodies—are freed, even while living, from the bondage that is birth, and go to the affliction-free station (pada), the supreme station of Viṣṇu called liberation (mokṣa).

How the commentary settled it

Where the verse alone is ambiguous, the reading follows Śaṅkara's commentary; the deciding lines are quoted below each decision.
Force of yasmāt/hi
→ Take yasmāt with hi causally: “for/since …,” governing the whole statement.
gita [2]hi yasmāt
“for, since/because.”
Syntactic connection of karmajam
→ karmajam modifies phalam, not buddhi-yuktāḥ: “the fruit born from actions.”
gita [1]karmajaṃ phalaṃ tyaktvā iti vyavahitena sambandhaḥ |
“The construction, despite separation, is: ‘having abandoned the action-born fruit.’”
gita [2]iṣṭāniṣṭa-deha-prāptiḥ karmajaṃ phalaṃ karmabhyo jātaṃ
“The action-born fruit, born from actions, is the obtaining of desirable and undesirable bodies.”
Sense of buddhi-yuktāḥ
→ buddhi-yuktāḥ means “endowed with the buddhi of equanimity,” not merely “intelligent.”
gita [2]buddhi-yuktāḥ samatva-buddhi-yuktāḥ santaḥ
“being buddhi-yukta means being endowed with the buddhi of sameness/equanimity.”
gita [3]athavā buddhi-yogād dhanaṃjaya ity ārabhya paramārtha-darśana-lakṣaṇaiva sarvataḥ saṃplutodaka-sthānīyā karma-yogaja-sattva-śuddha-janitā buddhir darśitā
“Or, from ‘buddhi-yoga, O Dhanaṃjaya’ onward, the buddhi shown is characterized by vision of ultimate reality, born of purity of sattva produced by karma-yoga.”
Sense of manīṣiṇaḥ
→ manīṣiṇaḥ is rendered “the wise/knowers.”
gita [2]manīṣiṇaḥ jñānino bhūtvā
“manīṣins, having become knowers.”
Compound relation and scope in janma-bandha-vinirmuktāḥ
→ janma-bandha is “the bondage that is birth”; vinirmuktāḥ means released from that bondage even while living.
gita [2]janma-bandha-vinirmuktāḥ janmaiva bandhaḥ janma-bandhaḥ tena vinirmuktāḥ jīvanta eva janma-bandhāt vinirmuktāḥ santaḥ
“janma-bandha-vinirmuktaḥ: birth itself is bondage; from that they are released—released from birth-bondage while still living.”
Meaning of pada and anāmaya
→ padaṃ anāmayam is the affliction-free supreme station of Viṣṇu, called liberation (mokṣa).
gita [2]padaṃ paramaṃ viṣṇoḥ mokṣākhyaṃ gacchanti anāmayaṃ sarvopadrava-rahitam ity arthaḥ
“They go to the supreme pada of Viṣṇu, called mokṣa; anāmaya means free from every affliction.”
