Bhagavad-Gītā with Śaṅkara's bhāṣya · 2.64

rāga-dveṣa-viyuktais tu viṣayān indriyaiś caran | ātma-vaśyair vidheyātmā prasādam adhigacchati

Translation. But one whose inner organ (antaḥ-karaṇa) is obedient to his will, while encountering unavoidable sense-objects with senses free from attraction and aversion (rāga-dveṣa) and under his own control, attains serenity (prasāda).

How the commentary settled it

Where the verse alone is ambiguous, the reading follows Śaṅkara's commentary; the deciding lines are quoted below each decision.
Compound relation and syntactic attachment of rāga-dveṣa-viyuktaiḥ
→ rāga-dveṣa-viyuktaiḥ qualifies indriyaiḥ: the senses are separated from the pair attraction and aversion; rāga-dveṣa is an internal dvandva, and the whole compound is a tṛtīyā-tatpuruṣa adjective.
gita [1]rāgaś ca dveṣaś ca rāga-dveṣau
Rāga and dveṣa together are rāga-dveṣa.
gita [1]tābhyāṃ viyuktaiḥ śrotrādibhir indriyair
with the senses beginning with hearing, separated from those two
Sense and object of caran with viṣayān
→ viṣayān caran means encountering or experiencing unavoidable sense-objects, not roaming in or indulging sense-objects generally.
gita [1]viṣayān avarjanīyān caran upalabhamānaḥ
moving among, i.e. experiencing, unavoidable objects
Meaning of ātma-vaśya and its agreement with indriyaiḥ
→ ātma-vaśyaiḥ means that the senses are under one’s own control.
gita [1]ātma-vaśyair ātmano vaśyāni vaśībhūtāni indriyāṇi tair ātma-vaśyair
ātma-vaśya: the senses are subject to the self, brought under control; with those ātma-vaśya senses
Analysis of vidheyātmā
→ vidheyātmā is a bahuvrīhi: one whose inner organ is obedient to his will; ātman here is antaḥ-karaṇa, not simply “soul.”
gita [1]vidheyātmā icchātaḥ vidheya ātmā antaḥ-karaṇaṃ yasya so 'yaṃ
vidheyātmā: he whose ātmā, the inner organ, is subject at will
Meaning of prasāda
→ prasāda is serenity or clear inner soundness, not divine favor or social grace.
gita [2]prasādaḥ prasannatā svāsthyam
prasāda is serenity/clarity, health or soundness
Force of tu
→ tu is rendered contrastively as “but.”

Śaṅkara's commentary on this unit

1rāga-dveṣa-viyuktai rāgaś ca dveṣaś ca rāga-dveṣau, tat-puraḥsarā hi indriyāṇāṃ pravṛttiḥ svābhāvikī, tatra yo mumukṣuḥ bhavati saḥ tābhyāṃ viyuktaiḥ śrotrādibhir indriyair viṣayān avarjanīyān caran upalabhamānaḥ ātma-vaśyair ātmano vaśyāni vaśībhūtāni indriyāṇi tair ātma-vaśyair vidheyātmā icchātaḥ vidheya ātmā antaḥ-karaṇaṃ yasya so 'yaṃ prasādam adhigacchati |
