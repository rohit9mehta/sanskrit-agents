Triṃśikā (Vasubandhu) with Sthiramati's bhāṣya · 18

sarvabījaṃ hi vijñānaṃ pariṇāmas tathā tathā | yāty anyonyavaśād yena vikalpaḥ sa sa jāyate

Translation. For consciousness—store-consciousness (ālayavijñāna)—is all-seeded (sarvabīja): by mutual influence its transformation (pariṇāma) reaches such-and-such states, and thereby this or that construction (vikalpa) arises.

How the commentary settled it

Where the verse alone is ambiguous, the reading follows Sthiramati's commentary; the deciding lines are quoted below each decision.
Reference of sarvabījaṃ vijñānam and the compound sense of sarvabīja.
→ “Consciousness” here means ālayavijñāna, qualified as “all-seeded” because it carries the powers/seeds for producing all dharmas.
trbh [1136]tatra sarvadharmotpādanaśaktyanugamāt sarvabījaṃ vijñānam ity ālayavijñānam
Here, because it is accompanied by the power to produce all dharmas, ‘all-seeded consciousness’ means ālayavijñāna.
trbh [1137, 1138, 1139]vijñānaṃ hy asarvabījam apy astīty ataḥ sarvabījam ity āha vijñānād anyad api kaiścit pradhānādi sarvabījaṃ kalpyata iti vijñānam ity āha
Since there is also consciousness that is not all-seeded, he says ‘all-seeded’; and since some imagine something other than consciousness, such as pradhāna, to be all-seeded, he says ‘consciousness.’
trbh [1140, 1141]atha vā ekapadavyabhicāre 'pi viśeṣaṇaviśeṣyatvadarśanān nāyaṃ doṣaḥ
Alternatively, even if a single word is non-exclusive, this is no fault, because a qualifier-qualified relation is observed.
Meaning of pariṇāma, force of yāti, and scope of tathā tathā.
→ pariṇāma is “alteration from a prior state,” and tathā tathā yāti means that this transformation reaches the state capable of immediately producing each respective vikalpa.
trbh [1142, 1143, 1144, 1145]pariṇāmas tathā tathā yāty anyonyavaśād iti pūrvāvasthāto 'nyathābhāvaḥ pariṇāmaḥ tathā tatheti tasya tasya vikalpasyānantarotpādanasamarthāvasthāṃ prāpnotīty arthaḥ
On ‘the transformation goes thus and thus because of mutual influence’: transformation is becoming otherwise than the previous state. ‘Thus and thus’ means ‘of each respective vikalpa’; the meaning is that it reaches a state capable of immediately producing that vikalpa.
Scope of “mutual influence/dependence” in anyonyavaśāt.
→ anyonyavaśāt is reciprocal conditioning: eye-etc. consciousness and the ālaya-consciousness transformation are each a condition (nimitta) for the other.
trbh [1146, 1147, 1148, 1149, 1150, 1151]anyonyavaśād iti tathā hi cakṣurādivijñānaṃ svaśaktiparipoṣe vartamāne śaktiviśiṣṭasyālayavijñānapariṇāmasya nimittaṃ so 'pi ālayavijñānapariṇāmaś cakṣurādivijñānasya nimittaṃ bhavati evam anyonyavaśād yasmād ubhayaṃ pravartate tasmād ālayavijñānād anyenānadhiṣṭhitād anekaprakāro vikalpaḥ sa sa
On ‘because of mutual influence’: when the nourishment of its own power is present, eye-etc. consciousness is a condition for the power-qualified transformation of ālayavijñāna; that ālayavijñāna-transformation too becomes a condition for eye-etc. consciousness. Thus, because both proceed through mutual influence, from that ālayavijñāna ...
Construal of yena, sa sa, vikalpaḥ, and the voice/sense of jāyate.
→ yena is rendered as correlative ‘whereby / by reason of which’; sa sa vikalpaḥ is distributive, ‘this or that construction,’ manifold and arising from ālayavijñāna without another governing basis. jāyate is therefore translated intransitively as ‘arises.’
trbh [1150, 1151, 1152]evam anyonyavaśād yasmād ubhayaṃ pravartate tasmād ālayavijñānād anyenānadhiṣṭhitād anekaprakāro vikalpaḥ sa sa jāyate
Thus, since both proceed through mutual influence, from that ālayavijñāna, not presided over by another, a manifold vikalpa, this or that, arises.
trbh [1153, 1154]tatra ca vartamāne janmani yathālayavijñānāt pravṛttivijñānasyotpattir bhavati tathākhyātam
And there, with respect to the present life, how the arising of active consciousness occurs from ālayavijñāna has thus been explained.
